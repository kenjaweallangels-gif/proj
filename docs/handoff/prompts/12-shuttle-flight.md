# 12 — Piloted shuttle: cockpit, harness, controls, realistic flight, docking, Moon

Goal: the player boards a shuttle docked at the station, straps into the seat, controls it from the cockpit with real spaceflight physics, undocks, flies around the station and Earth, accelerates and brakes, docks back, flies to the Moon and enters lunar orbit; collisions cause damage or explosions.
The shuttle must meet the same detail standard as package 04 (thick walls, no gaps, realistic surfaces) — design a plausible crew vehicle (e.g. a capsule+service module like Soyuz/Dragon class, or a small spaceplane), with a full cockpit interior: seats with 5-point harness (buckling animation), hand controllers (THC left, RHC right), panels with switches and MFDs (see MOCKUPS.md), windows with thick glass, lighting.
Physics: rigid body 6DOF with mass properties, RCS jets with real thrust/Isp/propellant and valve layout, main engine with gimbal, attitude modes (rate damping, attitude hold, prograde/retrograde/target), integration in ECI via ctx.universe (two-body + J2 + Moon + Sun, RK4/RK45), on-rails Kepler at high time rates, render frame following the shuttle (universe.setFrame) with the station placed relative to it; near the station relative motion must show correct orbital effects (CW behaviour). Felt control: camera shake and head motion with acceleration, harness restraint, engine rumble (package 06), g-meter.
Docking: undocking (hooks release, spring push-off ~0.1 m/s, back-away), approach corridor guidance (range, rate, lateral offset, alignment cross), contact and capture conditions (< 0.1 m/s, < 0.1 m, < 1 deg), latches; failed contact bounces; collisions with BVH (ctx.collision) -> damage model (panels dented, leaks, RCS failures), above thresholds explosion with debris and depressurisation.
Moon: TLI burn (~3.1 km/s), 3-day coast (time warp), LOI (~0.9 km/s), stable lunar orbit at ~100 km with the detailed Moon of package 02; navigation MFD shows orbit elements, apsides, burn planner (delta-v, burn time).
Acceptance: smoke checks for boarding/strapping, undocking, a 1 m/s burn changing apsides as computed, docking at low speed, crash damage, TLI to lunar orbit at high time rate; screenshots of cockpit day/night, approach view, lunar orbit; physics JS <= 1 ms/frame.
