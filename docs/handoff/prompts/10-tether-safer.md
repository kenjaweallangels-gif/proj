# 10 — Real tether with a reel, and SAFER maneuvering valves

Goal: a realistic EVA safety tether and suit maneuvering. Tether: braided steel cable simulated in vacuum (no air drag), slack and taut dynamics, wraps around structure without passing through it, retractable reel at the suit waist (ISS 85-ft safety tether reel style) with spring retraction, brake/lock, controllable reel speed and stops; the astronaut feels it (jerk when taut, pull toward the anchor). SAFER: 24 nitrogen thrusters (~3.6 N each) in the real layout, hand controller with translation/rotation modes, valve-level firing logic, AAH attitude hold, pulse vs continuous, ~3 m/s delta-v budget, visible cold-gas puffs from the actual nozzles.
Do: XPBD or position-based rope with 40-80 segments, bending stiffness, collision via ctx.collision sphere casts, constraint at the reel with variable length; keys: R reel in (hold, speed ramps), Shift+R pay out, Alt+R lock/unlock (show in MOCKUPS.md HUD), automatic retraction when slack; forces applied to the body (package 03) and audio events (package 06); SAFER firing table per axis, rotation couples, propellant use, HUD readouts (mode, valves firing, N2 pressure, delta-v left), particles from nozzle positions (package 05 provides the backpack model).
Acceptance: smoke checks (tether stops the astronaut at full length; reel-in pulls back to the airlock; SAFER translation achieves expected delta-v per second; AAH holds attitude after a bump); screenshots of tether paths around the module; physics <= 0.4 ms/frame.
