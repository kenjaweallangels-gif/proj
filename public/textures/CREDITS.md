# Texture and data credits

* `earth/earth_day_5400.jpg` — NASA Visible Earth, Blue Marble Next Generation with topography and bathymetry (December 2004). Public domain.
* `earth/earth_night_4096.jpg` — NASA Earth Observatory, Black Marble 2016 (resized). Public domain.
* `earth/earth_clouds_4096.jpg` — NASA Visible Earth, "cloud_combined" (resized from 8192×4096). Public domain.
* `earth/earth_specular_2048.jpg`, `earth/earth_normal_2048.jpg` — three.js examples (MIT), derived from NASA data.
* `sky/moon_1024.jpg` — NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, LRO LROC colour mosaic (CGI Moon Kit). Public domain.
* `sky/milkyway_2048.png` and `data/stars.bin` — generated by `tools/build_sky_data.py` from the d3-celestial
  data set (© Olaf Frohn, BSD-3-Clause, see `data/LICENSE-d3-celestial.txt`): 41 411 stars to magnitude 8
  (positions, magnitude, B-V colour) and Milky Way isophote contours.

Everything else in the scene (station, ships, suit, HUD, materials) is procedural and generated at runtime.
